# Example roles — the same engine, different worlds

The triage engine and safety contract don't change per role. Only the profile does — what
"your work" is, what matters, and how you're reached. A few sketches to show the range:

## Product Manager
- **Sources:** Linear (initiatives/projects they own), Slack (product + stakeholder
  channels), Notion (PRDs), calendar.
- **Matters:** decisions blocking a launch, stakeholder questions, at-risk milestones.
- **Urgent:** a launch-blocking issue, an exec asking for a status now.
- **Noise:** routine standup bots, individual PR approvals.

## Sales / Account Exec
- **Sources:** email, Slack (deal channels), CRM (HubSpot), calendar.
- **Matters:** a prospect reply, a stalled deal going cold, a contract question.
- **Urgent:** a hot prospect asking to talk today, a deal at risk before quarter-end.
- **Noise:** marketing digests, internal FYIs.

## Support / Success
- **Sources:** ticketing (Zendesk/Linear), Slack (support channel), customer email.
- **Matters:** an escalation, an angry customer, an SLA about to breach.
- **Urgent:** SLA breach imminent, a churn-risk account.
- **Noise:** auto-acknowledgements, resolved-ticket notifications.

Each of these is produced by the SAME onboarding questions — the person just answers for
their world, and the stand-in triages it. The two hard rules (never send, never
merge/close without a yes) hold for everyone.
